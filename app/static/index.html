Settings

    
Everything is free. Groq gives you a free API key with generous daily limits. When you hit a limit, the app switches to Ollama + Whisper on your PC automatically.

    

      

    

    

      
      
Get one free at console.groq.com/keys (sign in with Google and click “Create API Key”).

    

    

      
      
When one key hits its free daily limit, the app moves on to the next one, and long videos transcribe faster.

    

    

      

        
        
      

      

      
Order when picking clips: Groq, then this backup, then Ollama on your PC, then the built-in picker.

      
    

    

      

        

      

        

      

      

      

      

    

    

      
      
The app borrows your signed-in session from that browser to read links that need a login. It stays on your PC.

    

    

      
      

      
      
Finds the most-viewed Shorts about the same streamer and game, reads how they open, and picks and titles clips like the winners.

      
      
The best candidates get judged on what happens on screen (3 frames each, with Gemini or a Groq vision model). Action moves up, talk over a still screen moves down.

      
      
Every clip is checked for music (Gemini listens to it). Where a song plays, the music is removed and the voices kept, so the Short doesn't get blocked. "Restore sound" on a clip undoes it.

    

    

      
      
Gets the sound and a small preview first, then each picked or marked moment in full quality. A 6-hour stream takes about 2 GB instead of about 14 GB. If a part won't download, it gets the whole video instead.
